On Saturday, August 22, 2026, from 1 to 6 PM, we opened the doors at 11525 Stonehollow Dr. for the first time and invited Austin to come see where STEM education meets the moment.

What a day. Just over **1,200 people** came through the center, and together we raised around **$20,000**. Every dollar of that goes straight to our STEM education programs.

## What happened

Families toured all six spaces: the FIRST Arena, Wood Shop, Machine Shop, Electronics Lab, Makerspace, and Teaching Kitchen. Kids tried the hands-on activities, met the crew, and got a first look at what we're bringing to the community.

[Howdy Bots](/programs/howdy-bots), our FIRST Robotics Competition team, had their robot running in the FIRST Arena all afternoon, which is where the photo above was taken.

## By the numbers

- **1,200+** visitors
- **$20,000** raised for STEM education programs
- **6** spaces open for tours
- **1** very full ball pit in the FIRST Arena
